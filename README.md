## How to Use This Library

The 18 pillars are organized into four layers. Start with **Layer 0 (Self)** — it is the root of your derailer and the foundation everything else stands on. Then move outward: managing your **Team**, managing the **Business**, and managing your **Trajectory**.

```
LAYER 0 — SELF       → Pillars 11, 12        (your psychology & decisiveness)
LAYER 1 — TEAM       → Pillars 01,02,04,06,07,08,15  (the people you lead)
LAYER 2 — BUSINESS   → Pillars 03,05,09,10,13,16,17   (the org around you)
LAYER 3 — TRAJECTORY → Pillars 14, 18         (building the future)
```

## Anti-Caution Filter — Always Ask Yourself

```
1. Am I waiting for more data when 60% certainty is already enough to act?
2. Has the trading desk heard from me this week about what my team shipped?
3. Is my default response to a business question: defensive explanation or confident headline?
4. Am I protecting my team from visibility — or elevating them into it?
5. Is this "compliance" or "risk" — or is it my caution wearing a costume?
6. Am I optimizing my current role to perfection instead of demonstrating the next one?
```

## The 30-Day Anti-Caution Sprint

If you do nothing else, run these in order:

1. **Week 1 (Self):** Write your 5 personal decision principles (Pillar 12). Make one decision you've been sitting on (Pillar 11).
2. **Week 2 (Business):** Send your first 3-Win Weekly Dispatch to your MD (Pillar 10). Do one trading-desk friction check.
3. **Week 3 (Team):** Deliver one no-qualifier SBI feedback conversation (Pillar 01). Place each engineer in the Will/Skill matrix (Pillar 08).
4. **Week 4 (Trajectory):** Have one sponsor conversation (Pillar 18). Write your one-paragraph trajectory narrative.
