## Anti-Caution Daily Checklist

Before you close your laptop, run these six questions:

```
1. Am I waiting for more data when 60% certainty is already enough to act?
2. Has the trading desk heard from me this week about what my team shipped?
3. Is my default response to a business question: defensive explanation or confident headline?
4. Am I protecting my team from visibility — or elevating them into it?
5. Is this "compliance" or "risk" — or is it my caution wearing a costume?
6. Am I optimizing the current role to perfection instead of demonstrating the next one?
```

## Weekly Operating Cadence

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| **Monday** | Set 1 measurable sprint goal — make it visible to a stakeholder |
| **Tuesday** | Drive 1 cross-team dependency proactively — don't wait to be unblocked |
| **Wednesday** | Send 1 proactive commercial update to your MD or trading desk head |
| **Thursday** | Make 1 decision you've been sitting on — apply the reversibility test |
| **Friday** | Run 1 SBI feedback conversation + send your 3-Win Weekly Dispatch |
| **Ongoing** | Maintain your decision log, wins log, and promotion dossier |

## The 30-Day Anti-Caution Sprint

1. **Week 1 — Self:** Write your 5 personal decision principles ([Pillar 12](./12-self-leadership-personal-operating-system.md)). Make one decision you've been sitting on ([Pillar 11](./11-strategic-decisiveness-under-uncertainty.md)).
2. **Week 2 — Business:** Send your first 3-Win Weekly Dispatch to your MD ([Pillar 10](./10-executive-presence-selling-the-team.md)). Do one trading-desk friction check.
3. **Week 3 — Team:** Deliver one no-qualifier SBI feedback conversation ([Pillar 01](./01-performance-feedback.md)). Place each engineer in the Will/Skill matrix ([Pillar 08](./08-will-skill-matrix.md)).
4. **Week 4 — Trajectory:** Have one sponsor conversation ([Pillar 18](./18-career-trajectory-personal-brand.md)). Write your one-paragraph trajectory narrative.
